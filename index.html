Internal tool for managing our own YouTube channels

Powered by YouTube API Services

Shorts Factory is a private, internal command-line tool. It is used only by the operator to manage the operator's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and has no outside users.

What it does

  • Upload and scheduling: uploads videos the operator produced to the operator's own channels, as private videos with a scheduled publish time, and sets the title, description and tags.
  • Comment replies: reads new comments on the operator's own videos and posts replies as the channel. Replies are drafted with AI and saved to a log file before posting. Spam, ads and abusive comments are not replied to. Replies are spaced out in time and never posted in bulk.
  • First comment: posts one channel comment on each new video shortly after it goes public.
  • Own-channel analytics: reads YouTube Analytics reports for the operator's own channels to plan future videos.

What it does not do

  • It does not access any channel the operator does not own.
  • It does not sell, share or publish any YouTube data.
  • It does not like, subscribe, or comment on other people's videos.
